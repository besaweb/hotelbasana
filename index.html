A little closer to the sea

Where Albania meets the sea.

Hotel Basana sits a short walk from the sandy beach of Spille — a small, family-run hotel with around ten rooms and apartments, traditional Mediterranean architecture, and a restaurant, bar and beach area guests share and return to.

Mornings start slowly here. Coffee on the balcony, a walk to the water, an afternoon that doesn't need a plan. It's a straightforward kind of hospitality: comfortable rooms, a table by the sea, and Spille just outside the door.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay by card at the hotel?

Yes, you can pay by card at the hotel. We accept the common payment cards.

Do you have your own parking?

Yes, guests can use parking right at the hotel. We recommend confirming space availability when booking.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

No, pets are not allowed at our hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out until 10:00 AM. If you need a different time, contact us and we'll try to accommodate you.

Do you offer breakfast?

Yes, the hotel offers breakfast. We'll be happy to share the current offer and price when you book.

Is Wi-Fi free at the hotel?

Yes, Wi-Fi access is free for hotel guests.

How far is the hotel from the beach?

The hotel is located close to the beach. You'll find the exact distance and information about the nearest beaches in the Location section.

Can I book a room for one night?

Yes, a one-night stay is possible depending on current availability.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

A later arrival is possible by prior arrangement. Please let us know your expected arrival time when booking.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Cancellation terms depend on the specific reservation. The exact conditions are shown at booking or we'll let you know before your stay is confirmed.

How can I contact the hotel?

You can contact us by phone, email, or through the contact form on this website.
